The Text

Article XXVIII

The right of the people to copy shall not be infringed.

Why

If you've thought deeply about patents, copyrights, and trademarks, you've probably figured out that they are bad for progress and deeply unjust.

This campaign is organized around a simple long-term vision of passing a new Amendment to the U.S. Constitution ending patents, copyrights, and trademarks once and for all.

FAQ

What is the 28th Amendment?

There is not one yet. This campaign proposes one: The right of the people to copy shall not be infringed. The aim is ending patents, copyrights, and trademarks once and for all.

How is the Constitution amended?

Article V of the Constitution provides two ways to propose an amendment: a two-thirds vote of both houses of Congress, or a convention called by two-thirds of the state legislatures. An amendment is added only after ratification by three-fourths of the states, either in their legislatures or in state conventions, as Congress may direct.
